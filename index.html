Helper not found? Check in 3 steps:
1. Is the helper started? In the server-version folder run bash install-linux.sh (first time) or ./run.sh (Linux/Mac), or double-click run.bat (Windows). Wait until it says “Clip Vortex is running at http://localhost:8000” and keep that window open.
2. Open http://localhost:8000/api/health in a new tab. If it shows "ok": true, the helper is fine and your browser is blocking this site from talking to it (Brave Shields, Safari, or a denied "local network" prompt).
3. Easiest fix for step 2: use Clip Vortex straight from the helper at http://localhost:8000. It's the same site, already connected.
